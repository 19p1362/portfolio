The problem · 1 of 3

Four records, four owners

GP practice

Independent contractors. The lifelong record lives in EMIS Web or SystmOne.

Hospital trust

Its own electronic patient record, separate system and login.

Pharmacy

Its own dispensing record, often unconnected to the practice.

National

NHS number, Spine and GP Connect — plumbing, not the record.

So there is no single patient record. There are several — and the thing joining them is not a database.

The problem · 2 of 3

The join is a letter

Coding lag — the fact exists on paper, not in the record being searched.

Recall misses — a recall queries by code; free text is invisible.

Duplication — the same datum typed again into a second system.

The solution · 1 of 3

Fix the manual middle

1

Read

Letter or PDF in; diagnosis, medicine, action and dates out.

2

Propose

A SNOMED CT code per fact, with a confidence — not a decision.

3

Confirm

A clinician accepts, edits or rejects. Human, before the record.

4

File

FHIR R4 records on the NHS number, with an audit trail.

The machine does the reading. The clinician keeps the decision. Nothing is written silently.
