06
The target state

Stop filing documents.
Start filing facts.

One patient

Everything joins on the NHS number — not on a name typed twice.

One code

Each fact recorded in SNOMED CT, the terminology NHS care records use.

One trail

Who read it, who coded it, who changed it — and when.

The solution

Read the letter.
Propose the code.
Ask a human.

Photo or PDF becomes structured fields.

2 · Propose

Diagnosis and action, with a SNOMED CT code.

3 · Confirm

What it refuses
to do matters more.
