The problem · 1 of 2

Four records, one patient, no join

GP practice

Independent contractor. The lifelong record sits in EMIS Web or SystmOne.

Hospital trust

Its own electronic patient record — separate system, separate login.

Pharmacy

Its own dispensing record, often unconnected to the practice.

National

NHS number, Spine, GP Connect — plumbing, not the record.

So what joins them is not a database. It is correspondence: discharge summaries and clinic letters, arriving as documents.

HealthBridge2 / 6
The problem · 2 of 2 · diagram

A document is not a data point

Coding lag: the fact is on paper, not in the record being searched.

Recall misses: a recall queries codes; free text is invisible to it.

Duplication: the same datum typed again into a second system.

HealthBridge3 / 6
The solution · 1 of 3

Close the gap with four steps

1

Read

Letter or PDF in; diagnosis, medicine, action and dates out.

2

Propose

A SNOMED CT code per fact, with a confidence — not a decision.

3

Confirm

A clinician accepts, edits or rejects. Human, before the record.

4

File

FHIR R4 records on the NHS number, with an audit trail.

The machine does the reading. The clinician keeps the decision. Nothing is written silently.
