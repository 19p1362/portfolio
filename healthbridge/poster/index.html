The problem · structure

Four records, four owners

GP practice

Independent contractors, not NHS-owned. The lifelong record sits in EMIS Web or SystmOne.

Hospital trust

Runs its own electronic patient record. Separate system, separate login.

Pharmacy & community

Their own dispensing and care records, often with no direct link to the practice.

National services

NHS number, Spine, GP Connect, e-RS — the shared plumbing, not the record itself.

The problem · the consequences

What the gap produces

  • Re-reading — read once to understand it, again if it was coded wrong.
  • Coding lag — the fact exists on paper but not in the record being searched.
  • Recall misses — a recall query pulls by code; free text is invisible to it.
  • Duplication — the same datum typed into a second system, because they can't talk.
  • Delay — a result in an inbox is clinically the same as one that never arrived.

HealthBridge6 / 10
The solution · how it works

Four steps, one of them human

1 · Read

Take the letter or PDF. Extract the diagnosis, the medicine, the action, the dates.

2 · Propose

Suggest a SNOMED CT code for each fact, with a confidence — not a decision.

3 · Confirm (clinician)

Accept, edit or reject. Nothing enters the record before this step.

4 · File & export

Write FHIR R4 records on the NHS number, with an audit trail of who did what.

The solution · what it will not do

Three refusals

  • No autonomous diagnosis. The model reads; a clinician decides.
  • No shared patient pool. One practice cannot see another's patients.
  • No silent writes. Every filed fact is attributable and reversible.

And an honest limit: HealthBridge holds no UK certifications yet — DTAC, DSPT, DCB0129 and a DPIA are what a UK deployment would require, and slide-level claims don't substitute for them.
