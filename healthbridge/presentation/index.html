About us

Built for the work between the appointments

Who we are

HealthBridge is built by Gonela Abhilash — a CPC-certified risk-adjustment coder, registered pharmacist and MSc Public Health student at Anglia Ruskin. His day job is turning clinical records into data a system can use.

The problem we start from

A hospital writes one letter. A practice has to read it, decode it, code it and file it — often days after a clinician needed it. The letter is where care data goes missing.

What drives us

  • Fewer handoffs — one record, not six notes
  • Records the practice owns and can export
  • Change you can measure in a month, not a roadmap

Stated plainly: no deployed NHS customer yet. What follows is what we build and what we would measure.

Who it is for

GP practices and primary-care networks in England, community pharmacies, and small clinics moving off paper.

Services

What we do, and how it runs better

What we do

  • Read clinical correspondence — PDF or photo — and extract diagnosis, medication and action
  • Propose a SNOMED CT code for each fact, with the confidence shown
  • Require a clinician to accept, edit or reject before anything is filed
  • Run the visit loop: registration, vitals, consultation, prescription, pharmacy, follow-up

The human stays in charge of the clinical decision. The machine does the reading.

Making things run better

  • Find what slows the practice down — usually re-typing and re-reading
  • Remove the re-typing; keep one record per patient on the NHS number
  • Leave an audit trail: who read it, who coded it, who changed it

Faster, not busier.
The same people, doing less re-typing.

Digital, the right way

Paper to structured data — without breaking the practice

Going digital, the right way

  • Easier for patients — nothing new to learn at the desk
  • Simpler admin — one place a letter becomes a coded fact
  • Better decisions — trends and recalls that actually populate

Ingest → propose → confirm → file. Four steps, one audit trail.

Our focus clients

  • GP practices with a heavy correspondence inbox
  • Primary-care networks and PCNs sharing admin
  • Community pharmacies dispensing against those prescriptions
  • Small clinics still running on paper registers

Start narrow: one practice, one inbox, thirty days.

Why us · and what we measure

A pilot you can judge in 30 days

Why work with us

  • We understand clinical coding quality — it is the day job, not a side claim
  • No one-size-fits-all template; the workflow is set by the practice
  • Visible results: numbers a practice manager can read off one page
  • We listen first, then build — and we say what we cannot do

What we measure

  • Minutes per letter — before versus after
  • Codes accepted — share a clinician keeps unchanged
  • Recalls closed — by day 30, not rolled into next month

If those three do not move, the pilot failed and we say so.

UK deployment gates we do not hold yet: DTAC · DSPT · DPIA under UK GDPR · Cyber Essentials or ISO 27001 · DCB0129 with a named Clinical Safety Officer.
